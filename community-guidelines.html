Community Guidelines & Grievance Contact — Horn OK Please

Effective date: 22 September 2026 (grievance-contact section) · App: Horn OK Please (com.hornokplease.app)

Today (no multiplayer yet)

The current version of Horn OK Please has no chat, no nicknames visible to other players, no leaderboard, and no way for one player to see or contact another. There is nothing to moderate yet. This page mainly documents the rules that will apply once multiplayer features ship, so players and the developer have them agreed in advance.

Planned, once multiplayer ships (not in effect yet)

Nicknames. Territory generates a nickname for you automatically; you do not choose a real name or handle. If a future version allows customising it, nicknames must not impersonate another person or organisation, must not be offensive, and are filtered and reportable.

No open chat. By design, Territory and its planned alliances have no free-text chat channel in the versions currently planned — this removes the biggest moderation surface a solo-developer team cannot realistically staff. Any future addition of chat would come with its own moderation plan and an update to this page first.

Reporting. Once nicknames or alliance names are visible to other players, an in-app “Report” action will be available wherever another player’s name appears. Reports are reviewed by the developer (there is no separate trust & safety team at this scale); we may rename, restrict, or remove an account found to be impersonating someone, harassing another player, or otherwise misusing a name.

In-game “titles” are not real offices. Governance-style titles (for example “Sector Chief”, “Mayor”) are fictional and carry no real authority — see the Safety & Map Disclaimer. Using them to imply a real official role, or to harass another player, is a violation of these guidelines and of the Terms & Conditions.

Fair play. Location spoofing to fraudulently claim territory, automating play, and exploiting bugs for competitive advantage are not allowed and may result in a claim being reversed or an account being restricted once accounts exist.

Grievance contact (India, DPDP Act)

For any complaint related to personal data handling under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or a request under this page (report a user, a nickname complaint, or a question about these guidelines): email hornokplease.support@gmail.com, or open an issue at github.com/ajuneja1991/horn-ok-please-privacy/issues. We aim to acknowledge grievances within a reasonable time.
