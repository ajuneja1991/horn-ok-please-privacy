Safety & Map Disclaimer — Horn OK Please

Effective date: 22 September 2026 · App: Horn OK Please (com.hornokplease.app)

Play safely

Horn OK Please is a game. Do not use the app while driving, cycling, or in any situation where using a phone would be unsafe or illegal. If you play Territory while out and about, stop walking or find a safe place to stand before looking at your screen. The app does not require you to be moving, and does not reward moving quickly or dangerously — clearing a map cell rewards solving a puzzle, not travel speed.

Traffic and roads in this game are simulated

Every traffic condition, vehicle, and jam shown in Horn OK Please — including in Territory mode — is simulated for gameplay and is generated from a deterministic model, not from live sensors or live traffic feeds, unless a specific feature is explicitly labelled “live”. Nothing in this app is real-time traffic information, a navigation aid, or a substitute for checking real road conditions. Do not use the app to plan a real journey, avoid real congestion, or make any real-world driving decision.

The map is for gameplay, not an official reference

The India map, state boundaries and place names shown in the app are used for gameplay purposes and follow India’s official Survey-of-India-conformant external boundary as required for any India-facing map. Nothing in the app should be read as an official governmental map, a legal survey document, or a definitive statement on any disputed boundary or territorial matter. Where the app draws a distinction between areas under Indian administration and areas it is not (for gameplay unlock purposes only), that distinction changes nothing about how the boundary is drawn and is not a political statement.

In-game “ownership”, “governance titles” and similar terms are game mechanics only

Territory’s language of “capturing”, “owning”, “defending” a map cell, and titles such as “Mayor” or “State Governor” are entirely fictional game mechanics with no connection to real governance, real property, or any real office, political party, or individual. They confer no real-world rights, and holding one in the game implies nothing about the real place it is named after.

No warranty on map data accuracy

Road and place data used to build puzzle boards comes from OpenStreetMap contributors (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL licence) and may be incomplete, outdated, or simplified for gameplay. It is not warranted to be accurate or current. See the Privacy Policy for the full data-sourcing notes.
