Privacy Policy — Horn OK Please

Effective date: 19 September 2026 · App: Horn OK Please (com.hornokplease.app)

Horn OK Please (“the app”) is a puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app handles.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels completed, stars, coins, daily streak), your language and sound settings, and whether you own Pro are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts and no server of its own. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Advertising

The free version shows occasional interstitial ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK) the app asks for your consent before requesting ads, using Google’s consent tool.

You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Owners of Pro see no forced ads.

Google’s use of data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Analytics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase to understand how the game is used and to fix crashes. It records anonymous events such as which level was completed, how many moves it took, and whether a hint was used, plus crash and diagnostic reports (device model, Android version, app version, and the technical state at the time of a crash). Firebase also uses an app-instance identifier so events from one installation can be grouped; it is not linked to your name or account. The app also downloads remote settings from Firebase that control how often ads may appear.

Purchases

The optional Pro purchase is processed by Google Play. The app receives confirmation that the purchase happened; it does not receive or store your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices and requests

Because progress data never leaves your device, you can delete it by uninstalling the app. To ask about, access or delete analytics data associated with your installation, contact us using the link below.

Changes

If the app starts collecting additional information, this policy will be updated first and the app’s Play Store Data Safety section will match.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/ajuneja1991/horn-ok-please-privacy/issues.
