Privacy Policy — Horn OK Please

Effective date: 20 September 2026 · Applies to app version 1.0.2 and later; earlier versions are described where they differ · App: Horn OK Please (com.hornokplease.app)

Horn OK Please (“the app”) is a puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app handles.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels completed, stars, coins, daily streak), your language and sound settings, whether you own Pro, and your answer to the age question below are stored only on your device. If you use the Territory map (18 and over), the app also stores on your device the ids of the map cells you have revealed and cleared, a home cell id, the ids of the Indian states you have visited, and the powers and title you have earned. The app has no accounts and no server of its own in this version. Uninstalling the app removes all of this data.

Age question and child mode (version 1.0.2 and later)

The first time you open the app it asks “Are you 18 or older?” (yes, no, or skip; it does not ask for a date of birth). Your answer is stored only on your device and you can change it in Settings.

  • If you answer no, or skip, the app runs in child mode: it shows no ads, sends no analytics and no crash reports, does not use your location, and Territory is not available. The levels, Daily and Zen modes and Pro work as usual.
  • If you answer yes, the app behaves as described in the sections below.

Versions before 1.0.2 do not ask this question and behave as described below for everyone.

Advertising

If you confirm you are 18 or older (or you use a version before 1.0.2), the free version shows occasional interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK) the app asks for your consent before requesting ads, using Google’s consent tool, and you can change your choice on request.

You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Owners of Pro see no forced ads.

Google’s use of data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Analytics and crash reports

If you confirm you are 18 or older (or you use a version before 1.0.2), the app uses Google Firebase to understand how the game is used and to fix crashes. It records anonymous events such as which level was completed, how many moves it took, and whether a hint was used, plus crash and diagnostic reports (device model, Android version, app version, and the technical state at the time of a crash). Firebase also uses an app-instance identifier so events from one installation can be grouped; it is not linked to your name or account. The app also downloads remote settings from Firebase that control how often ads may appear. Where required, this follows your consent choice from the consent prompt. In child mode none of this is collected.

Location (Territory, 18 and over, version 1.0.2 and later)

Territory asks for Android’s precise or approximate location permission, after an in-app explanation and only when you choose to turn it on. The app uses your location only while it is open on screen. It never reads location in the background and never runs a location service when the app is closed. You can decline, and Territory then shows a demo map instead; you can withdraw the permission at any time in Android settings.

Your position is turned into a map cell and an Indian state on the phone itself and is held in memory only for a moment. The app does not save your coordinates and does not send them to us, to Google, to a map provider or to anyone else. What the app saves is the list of map cell ids (each a hexagon of about 0.7 km²), a home cell id and the ids of the states you have visited, stored on your device. Territory is for people aged 18 and over: if you say you are under 18 it is not available to you.

Maps and map data

The India map and the Territory cell map are drawn by the app itself from boundary data bundled with the app; drawing them does not contact any map provider. Some versions also show a street map. When it is shown, its picture tiles are downloaded from OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org), which can see your device’s IP address and which map tiles are requested, that is, the general area you are viewing. The app sends OpenFreeMap no coordinates, account or other data. Road and place data is © OpenStreetMap contributors and is available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL, openstreetmap.org/copyright). The India boundary and state outlines follow Survey of India published data, using the DataMeet dataset (CC BY 4.0), and are shown for illustration. Traffic shown in the game is simulated, not live.

Purchases

The optional Pro purchase is processed by Google Play. The app receives confirmation that the purchase happened; it does not receive or store your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information. Players under 18, and anyone who skips the age question, get child mode (see above): no ads, no analytics or crash reports, and no location.

Your choices and requests

Because your progress data stays on your device, you can delete it by uninstalling the app. To ask about, access or delete analytics data associated with your installation, contact us using the link below.
