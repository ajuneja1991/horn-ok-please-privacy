Data Deletion & Retention — Horn OK Please

Effective date: 22 September 2026 · App: Horn OK Please (com.hornokplease.app)

Today’s version: everything is local, so deletion is in your hands

In the current version of Horn OK Please, there is no account and no server-side copy of your data. Everything the app stores (progress, coins, streak, settings, Pro status, and — if you use Territory — your revealed/cleared map cell ids, home cell id, visited-state ids, powers and titles) lives only on your device (see the Privacy Policy → “What stays on your device”).

To delete all of your data: uninstall the app. This removes everything the app stored on your device immediately. If your phone’s Android device backup was on, a backup copy may exist in your own Google Account until it next syncs or expires — see the Privacy Policy’s “Android device backup” notes for how to manage that from your phone’s own Settings, since we do not have access to it.

Analytics and crash data: for players who are 18 or over and have not opted out, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics may retain anonymous usage and crash data as described in the Privacy Policy → “Analytics and crash reports”. This data is not tied to your name or contact details. To ask about or request deletion of analytics data associated with your installation, contact us using the link below — we will need enough detail (approximate date, device, app version) to attempt to locate it, since it is not linked to an identity we can look up by name or email.

Retention: local device data persists until you delete it (uninstall, or clear app data in Android settings) or your device is reset. Anonymous analytics/crash data follows Google’s standard Firebase retention windows unless deleted sooner on request.

When Territory is online (planned, not in effect yet)

Once Territory adds accounts (Firebase Authentication, optionally linked to Google Sign-In) and a server, this section will describe:

  • In-app deletion: a “Delete my account” row in Settings that removes your account, nickname, owned cells, level, and any stored claim history from our servers.
  • Web deletion: a web page (linked here) that lets you request deletion without the app installed, as Play’s policy requires once an account exists.
  • What is retained briefly for anti-abuse/fairness: server logs needed to investigate cheating or abuse reports may be retained for a limited window after a deletion request.
  • What is never retained: raw location coordinates are never sent to or stored by the server in any version — only cell ids, so there is nothing “location history” to delete beyond the cell ids themselves.

This section will be completed before the online build ships to any Play track, alongside the matching Data Safety and DPDP updates.
